## Overview

BJ’s operates membership warehouse clubs. At FY2025 year-end, it had 263 clubs and 199 gas stations in 21 states (10-K line 00190). Club and Club+ annual membership fees were generally $60 and $120. The company said its private-label brands represented about 27% of net sales excluding gasoline (line 00191).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

BJ’s says its warehouse model lowers merchandise handling costs by buying in volume and selling from the sales floor (10-K line 00194). In Q2 FY2026, comparable club sales grew 11.9%; membership-fee income grew 9.9% to $135.6 million; membership reached 8.5 million; and digitally enabled comparable sales grew 30% (earnings release lines 00011–00015). The release reported three new clubs and one new gas station (line 00017). These are Q2 figures, not Q3 figures.

Management's FY2026 guidance included adjusted EPS of $4.60–$4.80 and about $800 million in capital expenditures (release lines 00061–00062). These are estimates, not reported results. The report should not equate adjusted EPS guidance with GAAP EPS.
