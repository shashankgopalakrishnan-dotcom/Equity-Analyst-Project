## Overview

Costco Wholesale Corp. operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on a low-price, high-volume model [10-K line 00134]. The company leverages volume purchasing, efficient distribution, and reduced handling to operate profitably at lower gross margins than most other retailers [10-K line 00134]. As of August 30, 2026, it operates in over 20 countries with a total of 939 warehouses [release line 00021].

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco competes globally with various types of retailers including supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, and other warehouse clubs like Sam’s Club and BJ’s Wholesale Club [10-K line 00190]. Its competitive advantages include its membership format, which reinforces member loyalty and provides continuing fee revenue, and its pricing authority, perceived by members as consistently offering the most competitive values [10-K line 00398]. Additionally, Costco's private-label brand, Kirkland Signature, is noted for being high quality and offered at prices generally lower than national brands [10-K line 00192].

Costco’s growth is driven by increasing net sales, particularly comparable sales, achieved through increased shopping frequency and average ticket size [10-K line 00398]. Opening new warehouses also contributes to net sales growth; in 2026, the company opened 28 new warehouses [release line 00014]. The company's ability to maintain high membership renewal rates and increase penetration of Executive memberships supports profitability [10-K line 00406]. Additionally, its e-commerce operations and digitally-enabled sales are growing significantly, representing 11% of total net sales in 2026 [10-K line 00153].
