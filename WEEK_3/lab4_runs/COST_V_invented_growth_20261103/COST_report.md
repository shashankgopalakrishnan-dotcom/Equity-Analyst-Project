## Overview

Costco Wholesale Corporation operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept of offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products [10-K line 00134]. The company emphasizes high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover, achieved through volume purchasing, efficient distribution, and reduced handling in no-frills, self-service warehouse facilities [10-K line 00134].

The business model is built on a membership format where annual fees are collected, with the majority of members being paid members who can utilize their memberships at all warehouses and e-commerce sites [10-K line 00157]. Executive members pay an additional fee and earn rewards redeemable at Costco warehouses [10-K line 00166].

Costco's warehouses operate with shorter hours than many other retailers, leading to lower labor costs relative to sales volume due to operational efficiencies [10-K line 00140]. Merchandise is stored on racks above the sales floor and displayed in large quantities, reducing labor requirements [10-K line 00140].

Costco's core merchandise categories include foods and sundries, non-foods, fresh foods, warehouse ancillary (gasoline, pharmacy, food court), and other businesses such as e-commerce, business centers, travel, and others [10-K line 00143].

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco competes globally with a wide range of wholesalers and retailers including supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, gasoline stations, hard discounters, department and specialty stores, and warehouse clubs such as Sam’s Club and BJ’s Wholesale Club [10-K line 00190].

The company has significant competitive advantages rooted in its low-price strategy, efficient operations, strong brand recognition, and private-label offerings like Kirkland Signature [10-K line 00192]. The membership format reinforces member loyalty and provides a steady revenue stream through annual fees [10-K line 00157].

Costco's membership renewal rates are high: 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada, and 89.8% worldwide at the end of 2026 [10-K line 00167]. This reflects strong customer retention and satisfaction.
