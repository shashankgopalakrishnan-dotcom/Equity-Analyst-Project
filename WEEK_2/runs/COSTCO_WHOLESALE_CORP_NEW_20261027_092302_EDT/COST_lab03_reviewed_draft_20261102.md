## Overview

Costco operates membership warehouses and e-commerce channels. It sells a limited range of national brands and its Kirkland Signature private label. U.S. annual membership costs $65 (10-K line 00157). Costco says most warehouses carry fewer than 4,000 active SKUs (line 00141). Cross-docking and depots help move goods through its distribution system (lines 00135–00138). Digitally enabled sales were 11% of net sales in FY2026 (line 00153).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco reported a 92.3% U.S. and Canada renewal rate (10-K line 00167). Executive members represented 75.2% of sales; their reward is 2% on qualifying purchases (line 00166). Costco reported 28 warehouse openings, including three relocations, for 25 net additions: 18 in the U.S., five in Canada, and two in other international markets (line 00415). Net sales increased 10% (line 00416). This filing does not attribute all sales growth to new warehouses.

Gasoline sales added $3.501 billion, or 130 basis points, to net sales growth. Foreign exchange added $1.332 billion, or 49 basis points (lines 00454–00455). Costco received $184 million in IEEPA refunds during FY2026: $174 million reduced merchandise costs and $10 million was interest income. It received another $155 million after year-end (line 00426). The Q4 release says diluted EPS included a non-recurring $0.15 benefit from the refunds (line 00020).

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

Costco reported FY2026 net income growth of 14%, net-sales growth of 10%, and a 13% dividend increase (10-K lines 00416 and 00422–00423). Other International net sales grew 12%; U.S. net sales grew 10% (lines 00435–00438). Digitally enabled comparable sales grew 21% (line 00452). Sales in ancillary and other businesses increased by $10.199 billion, or 20%, led by gasoline and pharmacy (line 00453). This is an increase in those businesses, not total e-commerce or travel revenue.

Track membership renewal, comparable sales, warehouse openings, digital sales, and the effects of gasoline prices, foreign exchange, and tariff refunds. Separate recurring performance from the non-recurring $0.15 Q4 EPS benefit when reviewing earnings.
