## Overview
Costco Wholesale Corp. operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept of offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories [10-K line 00134]. The company utilizes a no-frills, self-service warehouse format designed for efficiency in selling space, merchandise handling, and inventory control [10-K line 00139]. Costco buys most merchandise directly from suppliers, routing it through cross-docking consolidation points or directly to warehouses [10-K line 00135]. The company operates with a membership model where members pay annual fees ($65 in the U.S.) and can utilize memberships at all warehouses and e-commerce sites [10-K line 00157].

Costco competes globally with global, national, regional wholesalers and retailers including supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, gasoline stations, hard discounters, department and specialty stores, and operators selling narrow ranges of merchandise [10-K line 00190]. Significant competitors in the U.S. include Walmart, Target, Kroger, and Amazon, as well as other warehouse clubs such as Sam’s Club and BJ’s Wholesale Club [10-K line 00190]. Costco's competitive advantage includes its low prices, quality merchandise selection, membership model, and operational efficiency [10-K line 00134].

The primary growth driver for Costco is increasing net sales, particularly comparable sales [MD&A line 00398]. Comparable sales are achieved through increased shopping frequency from new and existing members and the amount they spend per visit (average ticket) [MD&A line 00398]. The company also grows through opening new warehouses, with a focus on international markets where there is more room for expansion [MD&A line 00405]. Additionally, Costco benefits from its membership fee revenue, which increased by 11% in 2026, driven by new member sign-ups, fee increases, and upgrades to Executive memberships [MD&A line 00459].

Key catalysts for growth include the expansion of e-commerce operations, which represented 11% of total net sales in 2026 [10-K line 00153], and digitally-enabled sales, which increased by 21% in 2026 [MD&A line 00452]. Rising gasoline prices positively impacted net sales by $3.5 billion in 2026, contributing to a 130 basis point increase in net sales [MD&A line 00454]. The company's ability to maintain high member renewal rates (92.3% in U.S. and Canada) also serves as a catalyst [MD&A line 00459].

In 2026, Costco opened 28 new warehouses, including three relocations, for a total of 25 net new warehouses [MD&A line 00415]. Net sales increased by 10% to $297.2 billion in 2026, driven by an increase in comparable sales and sales at new warehouses [MD&A line 00416]. In April 2026, the Board of Directors approved a 13% increase in the quarterly cash dividend [MD&A line 00423].

Costco faces risks related to economic conditions including inflation or deflation, which can impact consumer spending and cost of goods [MD&A line 00398].
