## Overview

Walmart is a people-led, technology-powered omnichannel retailer. It serves customers through stores, eCommerce and related services (10-K line 00173). It operates Walmart U.S., Walmart International and Sam’s Club U.S. (line 00175). The 10-K reports more than 10,900 stores in 19 countries at the time of filing (line 00173).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

In Q2 FY2027, revenue was $187.9 billion, up 5.9% (earnings release lines 00019 and 00152). Global eCommerce sales grew 23%, the global advertising business grew 38%, and global membership-fee revenue grew 17% (lines 00020–00022). These are distinct growth measures. Walmart raised its FY2027 outlook for net sales growth to 4.0%–5.0% and adjusted operating-income growth to 7.0%–8.5%, in constant currency (lines 00014 and 00123). These are management estimates.

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act was enacted in July 2025. Walmart says its effects were not material to income-tax expense or its effective tax rate, though some provisions reduced FY2026 cash taxes (10-K line 00544). In Q2, tariff refunds increased reported operating income, while price investments partly offset the benefit (earnings release line 00024). Walmart said it planned to put remaining refunds into price investments.
