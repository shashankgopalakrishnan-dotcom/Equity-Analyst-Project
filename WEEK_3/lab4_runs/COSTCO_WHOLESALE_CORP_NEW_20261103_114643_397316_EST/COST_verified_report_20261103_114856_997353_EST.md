## Overview
Costco Wholesale Corporation operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites globally, offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products [10-K line 00134]. The company's core business model relies on a no-frills, self-service warehouse format with strict inventory controls to minimize shrinkage and reduce labor costs [10-K line 00139]. Membership fees are a significant revenue source, with annual fees of $65 in the U.S., varying in other countries, including a free household card [10-K line 00157]. In fiscal 2026, Costco reported net sales of $297.2 billion, a 10% increase, with comparable sales growth of 8% and membership fees rising 11% to $5.907 billion [10-K line 00451, 00456]. The company's strategic positioning is built on member loyalty, low prices, high-quality merchandise, and strong operational efficiency through volume purchasing and efficient distribution [10-K line 00134].

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
Costco competes globally with supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, and other warehouse clubs such as Sam’s Club and BJ’s Wholesale Club [10-K line 00190]. The company differentiates itself through private-label products like Kirkland Signature, which are often priced lower than national brands and contribute to higher margins [10-K line 00192]. Costco’s growth is driven by increasing net sales, particularly comparable sales, which include new warehouse openings and member retention [10-K line 00398]. In fiscal 2026, the company opened 28 new warehouses (including three relocations), resulting in 25 net new warehouses [10-K line 00415]. Digitally-enabled sales represented 11% of total net sales in 2026, indicating a strong push towards online commerce [10-K line 00153]. Expansion into international markets with higher growth potential and improved operational efficiency support long-term growth [10-K line 00408]. Recent catalysts include a 13% increase in the quarterly cash dividend approved by the Board of Directors in April 2026, as well as positive impacts from higher gasoline prices, which increased net sales by $3,501 million or 130 basis points in 2026 [10-K line 00423, 00454].
