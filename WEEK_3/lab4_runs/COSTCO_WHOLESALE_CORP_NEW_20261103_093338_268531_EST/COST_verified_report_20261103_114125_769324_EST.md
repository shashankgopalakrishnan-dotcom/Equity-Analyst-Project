# Costco Wholesale Corporation Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Operating Results

**Issaquah, Wash., September 24, 2026 - Costco Wholesale Corporation (“Costco” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: COST) today announced its operating results for the 16-week fourth quarter and the 52-week fiscal year ended August 30, 2026.**

## Financial Highlights

Net sales for the quarter increased 11.2 percent, to $93.9 billion, from $84.4 billion last year. Net sales for the fiscal year increased 10.1 percent, to $297.2 billion, from $269.9 billion last year.

Comparable sales for the fourth quarter and fiscal year were as follows:

| Period | U.S. | Canada | Other International | Total Company | Digitally-Enabled |
|--------|------|--------|-------------------|---------------|------------------|
| 16 Weeks | 10.7% | 5.0% | 7.0% | 9.4% | 19.5% |
| 52 Weeks | 8.2% | 7.8% | 9.8% | 8.4% | 20.9% |

*Excluding the impacts from changes in gasoline prices and foreign exchange.*

Net income for the fourth quarter was $2.998 billion, $6.75 per diluted share, compared to $2.610 billion, $5.87 per diluted share, last year. This year's fourth quarter was positively impacted by a non-recurring benefit of $0.15 per diluted share from IEEPA tariff refunds received in the quarter, less partial reinvestment of those refunds in increased member values.

Net income for the fiscal year was $9.226 billion, $20.76 per diluted share, compared to $8.099 billion, $18.21 per diluted share, last year.

## Company Operations

Costco currently operates 939 warehouses, including 647 in the United States and Puerto Rico, 115 in Canada, 43 in Mexico, 37 in Japan, 29 in the United Kingdom, 20 in Korea, 15 in Australia, 14 in Taiwan, seven in China, five in Spain, three in France, two in Sweden, and one each in Iceland, and New Zealand. Costco also operates e-commerce sites in the U.S., Canada, the U.K., Mexico, Korea, Taiwan, Japan, Australia, and China.

# Investment Recommendation
**Buy**

## Investment Thesis
Costco continues to demonstrate strong operational performance and financial health, driven by its effective membership model, efficient business practices, and strategic expansion. Key highlights include a 10% increase in net sales to $297.2 billion, with comparable sales growing 8%, and a significant boost from new warehouse openings and digital-enabled sales growth of 21%. Membership fee revenue increased 11%, supported by strong renewal rates (92.3% in U.S. and Canada) and membership upgrades.

The company's financial metrics reflect robust profitability and liquidity: net income rose 14% to $9.2 billion, diluted EPS reached $20.76, and cash flow from operations surged to $15.8 billion. Additionally, Costco maintains a strong balance sheet with low debt-to-equity ratio (0.17) and substantial cash reserves of $20.2 billion.

Despite a slight decline in gross margin percentage due to gasoline price inflation, the company's ability to leverage SG&A expenses through increased sales enhances overall profitability. The P/E ratio of 47.79 indicates a premium valuation, which is consistent with Costco’s strong performance and market position.
