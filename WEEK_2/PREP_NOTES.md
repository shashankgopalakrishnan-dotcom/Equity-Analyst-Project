## Francis & Zhang (2026), Section 3.1 and Figure 2

The workflow uses four coordinated roles. A Strategy Manager directs a Research Analyst, a Financial Analyst, and an Investment Advisor. The specialists work in sequence. The manager provides each role with a task, context, and expected output in structured JSON. It then checks completeness and consistency against a five-part report template and can request a targeted revision. This is a model workflow with specialized prompts and tools; the paper's described comparison does not isolate whether four agents outperform one model.

Figure 2 is useful as a view of the research process: qualitative company work, filings and financial analysis, then the investment recommendation. Keep the figure open when comparing the paper workflow with the Lab 03 program.

## Supplementary paper: Wang, Zhang & Zhang (2019)

**Title:** “Fire Sales and Impediments to Liquidity Provision in the Corporate Bond Market,” *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis*, 2019.

The paper studies corporate bond liquidity around insurance-company fire sales. It reports that mutual funds provide liquidity during these sales, but this provision falls after post-crisis rules. Fund capital constraints also limit liquidity provision. The authors report that funds in the highest liquidity-provision group perform better, which they interpret as evidence of skill. These are results in the paper's sample and design; they do not establish that a particular fund or security is a profitable investment today.

This PDF is supplementary background. It does not appear on the Week 2 assigned-reading list.
