## Overview

Target operates as one reportable segment, with stores and digital channels (10-K line 00139). It differentiates through design, style, value, and a curated assortment (line 00142). Stores fulfilled more than 97% of merchandise sales in each of the last three years; this does not mean that all those sales were same-day deliveries (line 00155). About half of merchandise was sourced outside the United States (line 00216).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

FY2025 digitally originated sales were 20.6% of sales (10-K line 00678). Target reported that sales in all six core merchandise categories grew in the quarter (line 00015).

The 10-K says the U.S. Supreme Court ruled on February 20, 2026 that tariffs imposed under IEEPA were not authorized by statute. The refunds added $1.65 to GAAP diluted EPS (release lines 00024 and 00026). This was a material, non-recurring benefit.

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

Target reported Q2 comparable sales growth of 3.8%, with store comparable sales growth of 2.7% and digitally originated comparable sales growth of 8.7% (release lines 00037, 00204 and 00210–00211). The 10-K reports FY2025 GAAP operating income was 8.1% lower year over year; adjusted operating income was 14.2% lower (line 00596). These are declines in income, not percentage-point changes in margin. The cited filing does not attribute the 14.2% decline to inventory-shrink improvements.

Target says it gives 5% of profit to communities (10-K line 00576). It reported no Q2 share repurchases and approximately $8.3 billion of remaining capacity under its authorization (release line 00046). Monitor net sales, comparable sales, operating income, tariff refunds, and whether digital growth supports profitable growth.
