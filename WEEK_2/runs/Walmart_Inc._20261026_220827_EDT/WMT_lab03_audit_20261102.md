## Other claims, citations, and recommendation

- The report says “Q2 FY27 results” and the 5.9% figure is correct. Its citations to release line 00019 should point to line 00008 for growth and line 00152 for $187.937B total revenue. Use line 00011 or 00170 for $0.80 diluted GAAP EPS.
- The FY27 outlook of 4.0–5.0% net-sales growth and 7.0–8.5% adjusted operating-income growth is in release lines 00014 and 00123. These are management guidance, not reported results. The report should retain “constant currency” where the release qualifies the guidance.
- The 23% global eCommerce growth, 38% advertising growth, and 17% membership-income growth are separate metrics. The first is in release line 00014; confirm the exact release lines for the other two before submission. They do not all appear in line 00020 as the report suggests.
- The OBBB Act claim is supported by the 10-K, but the release citation is wrong. Cite 10-K line 00544. The filing says the Act's impact was not material to income tax expense or the effective tax rate, though certain provisions decreased cash taxes in FY2026.
- “Fuel price adjustments impacting comparable sales” needs a precise source and explanation. The release discusses comparable sales and fuel separately; do not imply a causal effect without support.
- The valuation section reports WMT P/E of 43.71, which recalculates as `$119.32 / $2.73 = 43.71x`. Label it as a historical-EPS multiple. The report lists COST, TGT and BJ but gives no comparable peer valuation analysis.
- The Buy recommendation relies mainly on one quarter's growth and management guidance. It does not explain how the 43.71x P/E affects the decision, state a valuation range, or set out uncertainty. The recommendation is not fully supported by the valuation discussion as written.
