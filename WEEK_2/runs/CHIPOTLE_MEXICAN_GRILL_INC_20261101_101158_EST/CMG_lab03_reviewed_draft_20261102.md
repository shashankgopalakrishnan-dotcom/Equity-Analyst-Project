## Overview

In Q3 2026, total revenue was $3,293.162 million, compared with $3,003.444 million in Q3 2025; the release reports 9.6% growth (earnings release lines 00018 and 00070). ## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Chipotle describes its food-sourcing approach as “Responsibly Raised®” (10-K line 00188). In Q3 2026, digital sales were 38.8% of food and beverage revenue, compared with 36.7% in Q3 2025 (earnings release line 00027). The release reported 84 company-owned restaurant openings, including 53 Chipotlanes, and five international partner-operated openings (line 00024). It names Middle East and South Korea expansion plans (line 00061).

These openings and digital sales are growth indicators. They do not by themselves establish that future sales or profits will increase. The company reported nearly 90% of in-restaurant leadership roles were filled through internal promotion (10-K line 00209).

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

Q3 comparable sales grew 2.1%, with transactions up 0.8% (release lines 00019 and 00027). Operating margin declined from 15.9% to 13.7% (line 00020). Food, beverage, and packaging cost dollars rose 4.5%; the filing reports food-cost inflation of 0.4% (10-K lines 00517 and 00519). Labor cost dollars rose 7.2% (line 00526). The 10-K reports 36.7% digital sales for FY2025 (line 00205), while the release reports 38.8% for Q3 2026 (line 00027); keep the periods distinct.

The company said $1.6 billion remained under its repurchase authorization and it repurchased $110 million of shares in Q3 (release line 00035). These buybacks do not prove that they offset lower operating margin. Monitor comparable sales, transactions, operating margin, food and labor costs, restaurant openings, and buybacks.
