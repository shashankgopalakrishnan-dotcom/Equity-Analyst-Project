# Overview
Costco operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept of offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products [10-K line 00134]. This model aims to produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover, which enables the company to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins than most other retailers [10-K line 00134]. The company buys most merchandise directly from suppliers and routes it through cross-docking consolidation points or directly to warehouses [10-K line 00135]. Costco's business model also includes a membership format that encourages frequent shopping, with an annual fee of $65 in the U.S. [10-K line 00134, 00157].

# Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
Costco competes globally with a wide array of retailers including supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, gasoline stations, hard discounters, department and specialty stores, and warehouse clubs such as Sam’s Club and BJ’s Wholesale Club [10-K line 00190]. The company holds a strong market presence in multiple countries including the U.S., Canada, Mexico, Japan, U.K., Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland, and New Zealand [10-K line 00131]. It differentiates itself through competitive pricing, quality merchandise, limited SKUs (less than 4,000 active SKUs per warehouse), and its private-label brand, Kirkland Signature [10-K line 00141, 00192]. Key catalysts driving Costco’s performance include strong comparable sales growth, increased membership fee revenue, expansion into new markets, and favorable conditions in e-commerce and digital sales [10-K line 00414-00423]. Notably, the company reported a 10% increase in net sales for 2026, driven by comparable sales growth and new warehouse openings [10-K line 00416]. Additionally, higher gasoline prices positively impacted net sales by $3,501 or 130 basis points [10-K line 00454]. The company also benefited from IEEPA tariff refunds, which reduced merchandise costs and were partially reinvested in member value [10-K line 00426].
