## Other claims, citations, and recommendation

- The annual revenue citation `[10-K line 00139]` is not the statement line. Cite line 00626. Annual growth, net income, diluted EPS, ROAE, debt/equity, and FCF citations should point to their actual statement lines listed above, not general lines 00579, 00636, or 00677.
- The report says “5% profit to communities.” Verify the exact wording and whether the filing says profit or sales before using it. The current line citation does not establish that wording.
- The $994M tariff refund and $1.65 diluted-EPS benefit are correct. The release says $994M reduced cost of sales and contributed $752M to net earnings; it is a one-time benefit. Report this context beside Q2 earnings, not as recurring profitability.
- “Target Plus and Precision Plus” is not supported by the report's cited release line 00027. The release identifies Target Plus and Roundel/Target Circle 360 as drivers of non-merchandise sales growth over 20% at line 00017. Confirm each program name and keep the correct line.
- The 10-K does report a February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling that IEEPA tariffs were not authorized by the statute (line 00579). The report should also retain the filing's caveat: the ruling did not establish a refund process, and timing and recovery amounts remained uncertain. The later Q2 release confirms Target received $994M in refunds.
- “14.2% margin decline from inventory shrink improvements” is wrong. The 10-K says adjusted operating income was 14.2% lower, not that a margin declined by 14.2%; GAAP operating income was 8.1% lower (line 00596). The report also gives no source for the asserted cause “inventory shrink improvements.”
- The report's “approximately 97.6%” figure is too precise and is paired with the wrong meaning. The 10-K says stores fulfilled more than 97% of total merchandise sales, not that 97.6% of sales were same-day delivery (line 00155).
- The 20.6% digitally originated sales share is supported by the 10-K's annual channel table at line 00678. The report cites the quarterly release instead. Its claim that 5% of profit goes to communities is also supported, but the report should cite 10-K line 00576, not line 00139.
- The report states Q2 net sales grew 5.3% and digital sales grew 8.7%. The release calls 8.7% “digitally originated comparable sales” (line 00037), not same-day delivery growth. It separately says same-day delivery grew more than 25% (line 00014). The 20.6% digitally originated sales share is an annual 10-K figure (line 00678), not a Q2 release figure. Keep these measures and periods separate. The release also reports Q2 comparable sales growth of 3.8% (line 00037).
- P/E recalculates as `$148.01 / $8.13 = 18.21x`, using FY2025 EPS. Add the “x” and label it historical EPS. Peer comparisons are absent.
- The Buy recommendation uses the 5.3% growth, 8.7% digital comparable growth, and FCF. It omits the $4.11 quarterly EPS and the $1.65 one-time tariff benefit. “High” confidence is not justified by the mixed period and one-time-item evidence. Reassess the recommendation after including these points.
